Frequently AskedQuestions

Have another question? Reach out and we'll answer it directly.

What exactly does Avuem do?
Avuem designs and builds your website, then wires it into an automation system that captures leads, follows up instantly, and books meetings — so growth doesn't depend on someone manually checking an inbox.
Do I need any technical knowledge to use this?
No. We build, configure, and connect everything for you. You get a simple dashboard and notifications — the automation runs in the background.
Can this connect to tools I already use?
Yes. Avuem integrates with common CRMs, calendars, email, and SMS providers, plus most form and payment tools. If you're not sure yours is supported, ask us directly.
How long does setup take?
Most websites and core automations go live within 1–3 weeks depending on scope. We'll give you an exact timeline after a quick discovery call.
Is this a one-time build or ongoing?
Both options are available — a one-time build, or an ongoing plan where we keep optimizing your site and automations as your business grows.
